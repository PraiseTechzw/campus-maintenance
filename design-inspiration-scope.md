# Campus Maintenance Visual Inspiration Scope

The reference review will focus on **mobile service operations**, **maintenance request tracking**, **team work queues**, and **security alert handling**. It will assess transferable design principles rather than reproduce any individual layout, illustration, or branded asset.

The review criteria are: a strong operational header, quick scanning of urgent work, compact but information-rich job cards, elevated primary actions, restrained status color, evidence-preview presentation, and mobile-first navigation. Findings will be converted into original Campus Maintenance recommendations that preserve its deep-navy, operational-blue brand system and its accessibility requirements.
